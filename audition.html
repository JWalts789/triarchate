Casting Corvin

Four voices for our hollowed confessor — which one is him?

Each take reads the same lines from Chapter One, tuned grave and slow — a man worn down to a groove. Listen on headphones if you can; pick the voice that is Corvin, and I'll narrate the full chapter in it.

"The iron came out of the brazier the color of a sunset no one in the Ashes would ever afford to watch… Edrin screamed the way they all screamed, and Corvin's hand did not shake. That was the thing."

Voice A — British, grave

Ryan — measured, dignified, a weary authority. The institution made flesh.

Voice B — British, older & sadder

Thomas — softer, more tired, a man closer to breaking. The doubt audible.

Voice C — American, deep & measured

Christopher — gravelly newscaster gravitas; cold, controlled, hollow.

Voice D — American, low & weary

Guy — the deepest and most worn; a stone in the boot of a voice.
